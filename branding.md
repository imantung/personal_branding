# Personal Branding

## Position

**Technology and engineering leader applying AI pragmatically to real business systems.**

**Priority:** job search. Consultancy is a compatible extension of the same expertise, not the lead message here.

## Message

- **Lead with:** 15+ years in engineering; leadership of 30–50+ person teams; modernization and delivery.
- **Differentiate with:** current, hands-on AI work and a practical, cost-conscious approach to adoption.
- **Stay accurate:** ERPNext assistant is an early prototype; no users or measured outcomes reported.

**Headline**  
Technology & Engineering Leader | Modernization, Scalable Platforms & Practical AI Adoption

I’m a technology and engineering leader with 15+ years of experience across fintech, hospitality, travel, and on-demand services. **ERPNext AI Assistant — independent prototype**  
Developing a chat assistant that uses tools to interact with ERPNext and an intent classifier/router-dispatcher to direct requests. Also working on ERPNext UI/UX and Indonesian-language improvements. Early-stage; no user adoption or business outcomes claimed.

## Keep it consistent

- Put leadership outcomes before AI on the CV and job profiles.
- Separate implemented features from work in progress or plans.
- Add the project start date, stack, and demo when confirmed.
- Use the same dates and maturity description across CV, LinkedIn, website, and GitHub.
