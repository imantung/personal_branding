# Personal Brand SWOT

**Goal:** Focus on senior engineering leadership roles; let practical AI experience differentiate the profile. Keep the brand compatible with the consultancy, without making consultancy the job-search message.

## Strengths

- **Experience:** 15+ years in technology; led 30–50+ person organizations.
- **Domains:** Fintech, hospitality, travel, and mobility.
- **Known companies:** Gojek, Tiket.com, Koinworks.
- **Governance:** Regulated-fintech governance and risk background (AFPI Risk Management certification); ISO 27001 preparation at Koinworks and Bobobox.
- **Cost results:** Cut licence costs at Bobobox by up to IDR 100M per month.
- **Range:** Engineering depth plus org leadership, modernization, governance, and delivery.
- **Credentials:** Certified professional coach; Master's in IS Management.
- **Open source:** BaritoLog, adopted across Gojek.
- **Current AI work:** ERPNext chat assistant with a working intent classifier and tool calling.

## Weaknesses

- **AI maturity:** Early prototype (Jul 2026); no users or demo yet.
- **Few metrics:** Most outcomes are qualitative; only the Bobobox licence savings are quantified.
- **Short tenure:** Bobobox ~11 months, ended by a company-wide restructuring.
- **Employment gaps:** Apr – Aug 2024 and Dec 2024 – Jun 2025; explained in interviews, not on the CV.

## Opportunities

- **Positioning:** Engineering leader who adopts AI pragmatically.
- **Target roles:** Leadership in modernization, platforms, or AI adoption.
- **Proof:** Demo or case study of the ERPNext assistant, including architecture and limitations.
- **Differentiator:** Indonesian-language improvements, if demonstrated.

## Threats

- **Competition:** Applied-AI candidates may have stronger production evidence.
- **Credibility:** Overstating prototype maturity.
- **Mixed signals:** Leading with consultancy may confuse job-search intent.
- **Brand drift:** An AI-first brand could overshadow proven leadership strengths.
- **Past employers:** Koinworks has shut down due to a legal case; interviewers may ask about it.
- **Inconsistency:** Mismatched dates or project descriptions across profiles may raise questions.

## Takeaway

Lead with **engineering leadership and transformation**; make **practical AI adoption** the differentiator. Describe the ERPNext assistant as an early prototype—not a proven product or source of business outcomes.

## Best next proof

- Record the planned ERPNext demo video: workflow, stack, implemented features, limitations.
- Prepare short, neutral interview answers for the gaps, the Bobobox exit, and the Koinworks shutdown.
- Add metrics only after measuring them; align project status and dates across profiles.
